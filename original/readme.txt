First open prophet.exe and then interface.exe.

prophet.exe will create a sequence.txt with the order and interface.exe will show them.
You can customize your callouts with atheon.json and templar.json with the images attached.

For this to work you will need VAC https://vb-audio.com/Cable/ or something similar to route
the game's audio to a virtual microphone.

I will make a guide in the future but it is simple enough.

There are still some bugs like showing repeated notes on the second oracle wave but works
good enough to be helpful at this stage!
